Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. When this happens, we rely on adequacy decisions or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Blockchain data

Transactions on the Ronin blockchain are public and permanent. We cannot modify or delete data recorded on a blockchain, including your wallet address and the history of items linked to it. Please consider this before connecting a wallet.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and gameplay data: while your account is active, then deleted or anonymized within 12 months.
  • Logs and crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • AI companion conversations: as long as needed to keep your companions' memory of your save, and deleted with your account; Player2 may also retain data under its own privacy policy.
  • Data we must keep by law (e.g. tax records): for the legally required period.
